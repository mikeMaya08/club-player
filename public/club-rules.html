Baseline Tennis Club: Club Rules

Booking

  • Courts are booked in one-hour slots between the opening and closing hours.
  • Each member may hold a limited number of upcoming reservations at the same time.
  • Peak hours are charged at a higher rate. The price is shown before you confirm.

Cancellations

  • Reservations can be cancelled free of charge until a few hours before the start.
  • Late cancellations are not possible. Please contact the club office.
  • Players who do not show up are recorded as no-shows.

On the courts

  • Courts without lights cannot be booked from the evening hours.
  • Clean soles only. Clay courts must be swept after use.
  • Maintenance and tournaments may close courts. Affected reservations are cancelled and you are notified.

Lessons

  • Lesson seats are limited. Enroll early; you can leave a lesson at any time.
  • Coaches record attendance after each lesson.
